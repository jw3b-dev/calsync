calsync

A small personal tool that keeps John Wellard's own Google Calendar and Outlook.com calendar in step.

What it does

When an event is added, changed or removed in one of the two calendars, calsync makes the same change to a copy of it in the other. It runs every five minutes on John's own home server and looks at events from 30 days back to a year ahead.

It is not a service. Nobody else can sign up for it or use it: it only ever signs in to John's own Google account and his own Outlook.com account.

What it asks Google for

  • See and edit events on your calendars: to read the events in John's main Google calendar, to add copies of his Outlook.com events there, and to carry an edit or deletion made in Outlook.com across.
  • See the list of your calendars: to find that main calendar.

It never sends invitations or updates to anyone and never adds video-call links. It never changes or removes a meeting (an event with other people invited) on either side: meetings are only ever copied.
